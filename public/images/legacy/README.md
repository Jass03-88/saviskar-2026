## 🎨 Specifications for Drop-In Final Images

1. **Aspect Ratio**:
   - **Individual Dignitaries**: 4:5 or 3:4 portrait (Recommended: **800 × 1000 pixels** or **1200 × 1500 pixels**).
   - **Team DSA Group**: 16:9 landscape (Recommended: **1920 × 1080 pixels**).
2. **Format**: `.webp` (recommended for fastest web performance) or `.png` / `.jpg`.
3. **How to Update**:
   - Simply drop your final image file with the exact name above into `public/images/legacy/` or `public/images/dummy/`.
   - The Legacy Page (`/legacy`) will instantly pick it up.
